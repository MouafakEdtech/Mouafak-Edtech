Welcome to Word Builder!

A quick, 5-step tour before your first launch. Students spell a word by tapping letter tiles that fall into place under a picture. Let's set up your first deck.

🐘

1. Start fast with built-in Animals

Tap "Load Built-in Animal Vocabulary" to instantly add 16 ready-made animal words with pictures — no uploading required. Great for a first test run.

🖼️

2. Naming rule for every image

Whether you upload your own pictures or import a custom list, the same rule always applies: the PNG/JPG filename should be the word itself — e.g. banana.png teaches "banana". Underscores or dashes become spaces, so sea_turtle.png becomes "sea turtle".

📥

3. Bring your own word list anytime

Tap "Import New List" to load a whole folder of images at once by typing their filenames — perfect for building topic decks (colors, fruits, classroom objects) alongside the built-in library.

🎯

4. Toggle distractors, then press Start

Keep Distractor Letters on for a real spelling challenge, or turn it off for younger learners. Once at least one word is loaded, hit Start Game → and you're teaching.
